Local Man Found Dead In Forest

On the 3rd of January 1991, a local man (Aged 28) was found dead in Hellscape Forest.
Currently, the corpse has been evaluated to have approximately fifteen stab wounds across their body,
likely caused by a knife used by human hands (according to scientists testing on the body).
Despite this, the suspect of this murder has not been identified and no eye witnesses have been found.
If anyone has any information regarding the situation, please contact local authorities.
